# Medium modules

Read only the section that matches the deliverable.

## Carousel

- Treat each swipe as a transition, but allow a pause slide when it creates contrast or emphasis.
- The cover must work without prior context and at feed size.
- Vary density and composition according to the beat; avoid nine versions of one poster.
- Preserve navigation and mobile legibility.
- A framework or CTA appears only when the current brief needs it.
- Inspect both individual 4:5 or square pages and the full thumbnail strip.

## Presentation / deck

- Optimize for room/screen distance, presenter role, and speaking time.
- Separate what the audience reads from what the presenter says.
- Use section boundaries, orientation cues, and visual evidence appropriate to the argument.
- Do not turn every slide into a social-media cover.
- Inspect projection-scale text, charts, citations, and transitions between dense and sparse slides.

## Storyboard / short visual video

- Lock duration, aspect ratio, shot/keyframe count, motion ceiling, and whether audio is in scope.
- For each keyframe record action, camera/viewpoint, invariant, delta, transition, and timing.
- Motion must change information, relation, pressure, or feeling; pixel change alone is not story.
- Inspect identity, anatomy, material, lighting, and spatial continuity across frames.
- If audio is in scope, coordinate it; do not inherit a blanket audio exclusion from an earlier project.

## Still image / poster

- Create the narrative relation inside one frame through viewpoint, juxtaposition, traces, material behavior, implied before/after, or another brief-specific operation.
- Establish eye path and text-image division of labor.
- Inspect at both thumbnail and full size.
- A literal image is valid when literalness carries the desired force; a metaphor is valid only when its mapping holds.

## Document / visual essay

- Use page turns, section pacing, image placement, captions, and white space as narrative timing.
- Keep citations and source boundaries visible.
- Inspect full pages and consecutive spreads, not isolated assets only.
