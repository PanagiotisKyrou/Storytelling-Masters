# Master evidence ledger

Use this ledger to verify the source behind a master operation. The excerpts are short anchors, not slogans and not text to insert into the user's artifact. Open the linked source when wording, context, or attribution matters.

## Evidence policy

- `P` — the creator speaking or writing directly.
- `P-I` — the creator's words reproduced through a named intermediary; verify the cited publication/translation before formal quotation.
- `C` — a named close collaborator or production document.
- `S` — this skill's operational inference; never attribute it to the creator.
- A quote proves that the creator said something in a particular context. It does not prove a universal law or authorize surface imitation.
- Do not put a master name or quotation in the user's story, slide, or frame unless the user asks for it and the quotation is relevant.
