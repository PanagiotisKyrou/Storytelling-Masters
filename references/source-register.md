# Source Register

Use this register to keep evidence provenance visible. `P` means the creator's own words or an official first-party statement. `P-I` means the creator's words reproduced through a named intermediary or hosted copy. `C` means a close collaborator or production document. `O` means observation of finished work. `S` means this skill's synthesis; it must not be attributed to the creator. Short verified excerpts, context, and use limits are in [master-evidence-ledger.md](master-evidence-ledger.md).

## Boundary

These sources support craft operations, not imitation. The operational protocols in the dossiers are `S`: designed for this skill, challengeable, and subordinate to the current brief.
