# Source Register

Use this register to keep evidence provenance visible. `P` means the creator's own words or an official first-party statement. `C` means a close collaborator or production document. `O` means observation of finished work. `S` means this skill's synthesis; it must not be attributed to the creator.

## Boundary

These sources support craft operations, not imitation. The operational protocols in the dossiers are `S`: designed for this skill, challengeable, and subordinate to the current brief.
