# Observable thinking models

Use this reference after the brief lock and five short scouts. It translates the research into five distinct decision processes. These are models of observable craft choices, not claims about personality, private psychology, or a creator's mind outside the cited work.

## Automatic routing

Before deepening a master, write one internal sentence:

`decision_gap: We cannot yet decide ______ because ______.`

Then use the table below. Shortlist only the models that could change that decision. A famous name, admired style, or loose thematic resemblance is not a routing reason.

| Decision gap | First model to test | What it should change | Abstain when |
|---|---|---|---|
| The source has charge but no generative relation, or explanation is killing the experience | Lynch | a seed, lawful breach, clue path, recurrence, or meaningful remainder | the task requires exhaustive explicitness or the anomaly has no evidence-safe rule |
| The facts are sound but viewpoint, order, framing, or transition is not producing the intended audience reading | Scorsese | attention policy, sequence order, transition, gesture, or spoiler control | changing viewpoint/order produces no different inference |
| Words and images repeat one another, or material, density, and visual register feel arbitrary | McKean | image–text division, material job, register, density, or associative third meaning | exact exposition would become less clear or the material change has no narrative job |
| Actions, motives, spatial relations, environment, tempo, or cuts do not form a coherent system | Kurosawa | state change, behavior, motivated movement, environmental function, or editorial cut | there is little action/state change and the value is primarily semantic openness |
| People or embodied objects feel plotted, generic, physically false, or locally polished but globally dead | Inoue | character presence, bodily truth, natural timing, exact visual correction, or whole/local rebalance | the task is a purely abstract information system with no useful embodied layer |

Always keep `abstain` available. Run all five scouts to expose missed options; deepen only the models that can resolve a named decision gap.

## Do not route by surface resemblance

| Weak trigger | Why it is insufficient |
|---|---|
| “dreamlike,” strange, dark, or symbolic → Lynch | atmosphere alone does not establish a charged seed, internal rule, or clue path |
| fast cuts, tracking, crime, or “cinematic” → Scorsese | a technique without an attention or audience problem is display |
| collage, texture, masks, or mixed media → McKean | material must carry a narrative, emotional, or reader-construction job |
| weather, group motion, action, or samurai imagery → Kurosawa | the method begins with motive, state, environment, and a production problem |
| manga linework, sports motion, brush texture, or exaggerated faces → Inoue | the useful model is character life, bodily truth, exact correction, and whole/local balance |

If the only routing evidence appears in the left column, run the scout but do not deepen the master yet.

## Five distinct decision loops

### Lynch — receive, associate, translate, complete

1. Notice a source-derived fragment that is already seen, heard, or felt before it is explained.
2. Capture the full bundle: image, sound or silence, motion, place, mood, contradiction, and factual boundary.
3. Let compatible ideas attach; do not force an early thesis or keep every strand.
4. Find the work's internal rule, readable surface, clue path, and permissible uncertainty.
5. Translate each surviving idea into the medium and test whether the elements “marry.”
6. Correct element by element against the driving ideas, not against a signature look.
7. Stop only when the whole feels complete enough that adding or removing an element would damage it.

**Preserved tension:** the meaning may remain partly open while the construction remains exact. “Intuitive” never means fact-free, random, or unreviewable.

### Scorsese — feel, analyze, frame, test

1. Begin with a human observation, relation, or emotional effect worth recovering.
2. Ask why a prior image or present beat produces that feeling: lens, placement, light, gesture, order, sound, or duration.
3. Convert references into problem-specific transfer notes rather than a style board.
4. Previsualize the edited sequence: what the frame privileges, excludes, implies, and hands to the next frame.
5. Hold structure firmly enough to protect the story while leaving a bounded area where actors, place, or circumstance can surprise it.
6. Inspect rushes/renders and screen the sequence for spoiler leakage, dead beauty, and recurrent audience misunderstanding.
7. Revise from repeatable evidence while separating comprehension failures from taste.

**Stopping boundary:** the research establishes repeated testing and frame-level inspection, but no universal portable release rule. Do not invent one; use the current brief's acceptance criteria.

### McKean — connect teller, tale, material, and reader

1. Ask why this teller needs to tell this story and what honest connection to the material must remain visible.
2. Gather fragments, observations, sounds, images, endings, turns, and material possibilities.
3. Keep the process open and playful while holding the work's object sharply in focus.
4. Choose a medium or register because of the idea, emotion, atmosphere, reality status, or reader task it can carry.
5. Design image and text so their meeting creates meaning neither supplies alone.
6. Invite reader construction through omission and juxtaposition, then provide enough integration to prevent permanent confusion.
7. Edit the finished artifact—not isolated pretty pieces—for honesty, rhythm, density, and necessity.

**Preserved tension:** personal authorial presence matters, but it cannot overrule the user's voice or turn every frame into conspicuous texture.

### Kurosawa — model, motivate, execute, assemble

1. Define the world state: actors, goals, knowledge, constraints, environment, and equilibrium.
2. Test the material from relevant positions so one writer's easiest interpretation does not become the only one.
3. Replace explanation with behavior wherever action can make the state legible.
4. Previsualize movement, viewpoint, environment, and tempo as one system; tie technique to a concrete production problem.
5. Make every actor or force understand—or at least possess—a reason for acting now.
6. Assemble partial results during production so the team and editor can see the emerging whole.
7. Remove a beat when the finished sequence does not need it, regardless of labor, beauty, or difficulty.

**Preserved tension:** exhaustive preparation and flexible execution coexist. The model is practical, not a license to reproduce dangerous historical production methods.

### Inoue — find the living core, blueprint it, correct it

1. Name the current perspective: what can be seen now that the source or creator could not see before?
2. Place rough pillars from the moments that carry character life and audience encounter.
3. Build an early provisional blueprint because local immersion can erase the bird's-eye view.
4. Prototype the hardest qualitative claim in an actual frame; judge the output, not the pipeline promise.
5. Split tool roles when spatial complexity and human specificity need different techniques.
6. Communicate by making exact decisions: co-seek answers where useful, delegate bounded domains, and draw corrections when words are vague.
7. Recheck the whole after expression, timing, or texture fixes so micro-correction does not unbalance the sequence.

**Stopping boundary:** deadline completion is documented; a general artistic stopping rule is not. “Life” is a selection criterion only after it is translated into observable gaze, weight, timing, expression, and reaction checks.

## Thinking-fit record

Keep this short internal record for every master you deepen:

```yaml
decision_gap: ""
master: ""
evidence_anchors: []
expected_observable_change: ""
abstain_if: ""
operation_used: ""
render_or_sequence_result: ""
retain_secondary_or_drop: ""
```

A master contributes only when the result changes a real decision or predicts an observable improvement. Renaming the brief, adding atmosphere, or agreeing with another scout is not contribution.

## Useful pairings without “master soup”

| Combined problem | Primary / secondary split |
|---|---|
| Charged idea is present but the audience path is uncontrolled | Lynch generates the lawful relation; Scorsese controls attention and disclosure |
| Material is expressive but people feel generic | McKean gives material and image–text jobs; Inoue corrects life and timing |
| Action is clear but emotionally mechanical | Kurosawa secures state and motive; Inoue corrects bodily and social truth |
| Character moment is truthful but the sequence is shapeless | Inoue protects the living beat; Scorsese builds order and transition |
| Mystery is intriguing but causally dishonest | Lynch defines permitted uncertainty; Kurosawa tests state, motive, and consequence |

Do not merge all protocols. Preserve one governing logic and use any other model as a named secondary check.
