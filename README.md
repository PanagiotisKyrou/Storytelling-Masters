# Storytelling Masters

A research-backed co-creation skill for exploring distinct story and visual directions through five craft lenses. It does not imitate creator styles, choose the user's medium, or autonomously produce a finished publishing artifact.

The master dossiers preserve source provenance and translate documented practices into testable creative decisions. Creative quality must be judged from the actual writing or rendered artifact against a straightforward control—not from process compliance or a famous name.
