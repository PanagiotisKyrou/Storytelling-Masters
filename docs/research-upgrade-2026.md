# Research upgrade — August 2026

This upgrade replaces broad master summaries with traceable evidence, explicit source limits, and concrete creation/review instruments.

## Method

- Prefer creator interviews, official statements, official credits, autobiographical notes, and production documents.
- Use collaborator evidence for production facts.
- Exclude quote aggregators, fan captions, social quote cards, and untraceable aphorisms.
- Separate short creator quotations from skill synthesis.
- Preserve translation, hosting, and disagreement caveats.
- Convert evidence into a decision instrument only when it changes a present story or frame decision.

The compact quote/context record is in [`references/master-evidence-ledger.md`](../references/master-evidence-ledger.md). Full source classes are in [`references/source-register.md`](../references/source-register.md).

## What changed

### David Lynch

Direct interviews support charged multimodal ideas, clues, recurrence, rhythm, and internally lawful strange worlds. The dossier now separates required facts from an optional unresolved remainder and rejects accidental generation defects as “mystery.” Personal number games are explicitly non-transferable.

Primary sources: [Criterion](https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/3771-lynch-on-mulholland-dr), [*Film Comment*](https://www.filmcomment.com/article/out-to-lynch-david-lynch-interview-blue-velvet/), [*AnOther*](https://www.anothermag.com/design-living/8255/david-lynch-on-memory-chance-and-intuition), [*ArtReview*](https://artreview.com/daydreaming-is-so-important-to-me-how-david-lynch-fishes-for-ideas/).

### Martin Scorsese

Direct interviews support attention as visual philosophy, motivated gesture and movement, storyboarded transitions, restraint in sound/technique, external references with specific production functions, and repeated audience testing. The dossier now uses a frame-intent record rather than a generic “cinematic” prompt.

Primary sources: [National Endowment for the Humanities](https://www.neh.gov/humanities/2013/julyaugust/conversation/the-art-martin-scorsese), [The Film Foundation](https://www.film-foundation.org/the-persisting-vision), and a [hosted copy of the 2011 storyboarding catalogue interview](https://www.academia.edu/129663046/On_Storyboarding_Conversation_with_Martin_Scorsese).

### Dave McKean

Direct evidence supports choosing material for the story, conditional density, structurally necessary images, improvisation that still develops an ending/turn/core, and temporary ambiguity with recoverable relations. His explicit opposition to generative AI is now preserved as a disagreement and adversarial grounding test rather than hidden.

Primary sources: [official FAQ](https://www.davemckean.com/personal/faq/), [*The Comics Journal*](https://www.tcj.com/i-will-always-choose-reality-dave-mckean-retrospective/), [*The Beat*](https://www.comicsbeat.com/interview-dave-mckean-talks-raptor-and-embracing-chaos-in-his-process/), [*Publishers Weekly*](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industry-news/comics/article/86818-dave-mckean-tells-a-complex-dreamlike-story-in-raptor.html).

### Akira Kurosawa

The strongest direct words available online are reproduced by an independent site and explicitly attributed to *Something Like an Autobiography* (Audie E. Bock translation) and Toho's 1975 advice notes. They support character autonomy, behavior over explanation, tempo variation, multiple perspectives during writing, motivated action, technique as a production solution, and editing without sunk-cost protection. These are now labeled `P-I`, not official-estate primary pages.

Sources: [screenplay](https://akirakurosawa.info/akira-kurosawa-quotes/kurosawa-quotes-screenplay/), [camera](https://akirakurosawa.info/akira-kurosawa-quotes/kurosawa-quotes-camera/), [actors](https://akirakurosawa.info/akira-kurosawa-quotes/kurosawa-quotes-actors/), [editing](https://akirakurosawa.info/akira-kurosawa-quotes/kurosawa-quotes-editing/), and [Criterion's archive overview](https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/4471-from-the-kurosawa-archives).

### Takehiko Inoue

The official *First Slam Dunk* material is substantially stronger than the unofficial translated interviews used earlier. It supports rough story pillars, the need for an early blueprint and bird's-eye review, changed perspective with age, natural acting, paper-like texture, CG for ten-player motion, a 2D-like finish, manual correction of expressions/timing, and approval through concrete pilot imagery. Weak body-axis and “person over plot” claims were removed from the core.

Primary/production sources: [Inoue's official statement](https://itplanning.co.jp/en/inoue/i221020-2/), [official film credits](https://slamdunk-movie.jp/en/about/staff/), and [the distributor press kit](https://bigrednerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/slam_press-kit_2023-06-26_v2.pdf).

## Validation boundary

The research tests check evidence anchors, provenance boundaries, and whether the five documented decision models remain distinct. They do not certify story quality, visual quality, or successful transfer to a new brief.

Creative use must be tested against a straightforward control at comparable finish. The actual writing or rendered artifact—not a process record—decides whether a master-derived operation added value.
